Heir Vault

A dead man's switch that runs itself. If you go silent, Reactive Network hands your vault to your heir: no server, no keeper.

Check a vault

Anyone can look up a vault; you don't need a wallet. To ping, deposit or change the heir, connect the owner's wallet.

Create a vault

Testnet only (Ethereum Sepolia + Reactive Lasna). Your wallet signs every step; this page never sees your keys.

  1. Predict the watcher's address on Reactive Lasna
  2. Deploy the vault on Sepolia (signs once, sends your deposit)
  3. Get lREACT if needed (sends 0.05 SepETH to Reactive's faucet)
  4. Deploy the watcher on Lasna (subscribes to your vault and CRON)
  5. First heartbeat: ping the vault

How it works

The vault on Sepolia emits Heartbeat every time you ping. A reactive contract on Reactive Network listens to those heartbeats and to Reactive's built-in CRON. When you have been silent longer than your period, it calls release() on the vault, and only Reactive's callback proxy can do that. The vault re-checks the deadline itself and sends everything to your heir. Failed callbacks are retried; once released, the watcher unsubscribes so it stops paying for checks. Source and tests.
